# Pre-Origin Flow

## What it is

The spectrum that exists before black and white — before origin and shadow.
Pre-black and pre-white are not opposites; they are the source conditions from
which both draw. The spectrum between them is not existence — it is the
substrate of existence. Existence (the black-to-white range) is what
capillary structures draw upward from this pre-spectrum.

Analogues: the quantum foam before particles. The silence before sound.
The field before the standing wave. The pre-photonic before light collapses
into a photon.

Related search concepts: Fibonacci structure in pre-form states, dark energy
as a pre-black condition, zero-point field, standing wave as the first
distinguishable pattern from pre-form.

## Why it matters

Every system in radix has an origin — a point where it became nameable.
But before that naming, there is a pre-state. This concept names the
pre-state and gives it structure. Without it, we treat origins as absolute
beginnings. With it, we can trace the capillary path that drew the concept
upward into form.

This matters for the concept system specifically: when a concept enters
shadow, it is already partially formed. Pre-origin flow names what happened
before shadow entry — the formless pressure that eventually crystallized
into a concept worth naming.
